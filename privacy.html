Privacy Policy

NextPoint. Effective 16 September 2026.

NextPoint collects nothing. There is no account, no sign in, no server, no analytics and no third party code of any kind in the app. Every match you score stays on your own devices.

What NextPoint stores, and where

Each match is kept as a small text file on your iPhone, with the sport, the format, the two names you typed, every point in the order it was played, and a note or a photo if you added one. Your rivals, your presets and your settings live beside it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

iCloud

If you turn iCloud on in You, your matches, rivals and presets are copied to your own private iCloud database so your other devices can show them. That database belongs to your Apple Account and only your devices can read it; NextPoint has no access to it and no server of its own. Turn iCloud off and the files on each device stay exactly as they are.

Apple Health

When a match is scored on Apple Watch, NextPoint saves it to Apple Health as a workout of its sport, and reads the workout's heart rate, active energy and distance back to write the effort row on that match's report. Those are the only types NextPoint asks for. Nothing read from Health leaves the device, and what NextPoint may read or write is decided by you in the Health app, under Sharing.

Notifications

The one notification NextPoint sends tells you a monthly report is ready. It is scheduled by iOS on your device and carries no score. There is no push server.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. NextPoint never sees your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. It receives only whether a purchase is active.

What leaves your phone, and only when you ask

Three things, each started by you: a share card, an exported match, and the monthly report. NextPoint makes the file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice, and NextPoint is not involved.

Children

NextPoint is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing held about you to access, correct or delete. Your matches are on your devices and in your own iCloud, under your control: export them, or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and any change that affects what NextPoint collects will ship alongside an updated App Store privacy label.

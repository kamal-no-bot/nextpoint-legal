NextPoint Support

The umpire on your wrist: a score keeper for sixteen sports, with an Apple Watch app that scores a match without the phone.

Get in touch

Email engr.kamalnasir@gmail.com. A real person reads it, usually within two working days. Tell me your iOS and watchOS versions, the sport and the format you were scoring, and what you were doing when it went wrong; a screenshot of the report helps most.

Common questions

The score is not what I expected

Open the match report and read the point by point sheet: every point is there in the order it was scored, and the call the rules required at each one. If a point was scored to the wrong side, the crown on the watch or the undo on the phone takes it back. If you believe the rule itself was applied wrongly, send me the report and the sport; the rules library in the app names the edition NextPoint follows.

My watch says the matches are spent

NextPoint starts you with a small number of matches, counted the moment a match starts, on the phone or the watch. The full app has no count on it, and a purchase made on the phone reaches the watch the next time the two are near each other. If it has not, open NextPoint on the phone once.

Where is the effort row

The effort row on a report comes from the workout the watch saves to Apple Health. A match scored on the phone has no workout behind it. If a watch match shows no effort, check that NextPoint may read heart rate and active energy in the Health app, under Sharing.

Restoring a purchase

Open You, then the purchase card, and tap Restore purchases at the foot of the screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to a device.

iCloud

iCloud sync comes with the full app and is off until you turn it on in You. It copies your matches, rivals and presets to your own private iCloud database, and nowhere else.
